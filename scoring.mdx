Every player has a running **total score** (shown as `pts` under their seat). Scores change only when a round ends, and always by the round's final Confidence value.<br /><br />Does it autocommit? ??? ?????

## Round outcomes

### Win by fold-out

If everyone else folds, the last active player gains the current Confidence value. Nobody loses anything.

| Player | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Last player standing | **\+Confidence** |
| Everyone who folded | 0 |

### Win by showdown

When a showdown triggers, each active player's hand is solved from their 2 hole cards plus the 5 community cards (best 5 of 7) using the `pokersolver` library. `Hand.winners` picks the best hand(s):

| Player | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Showdown winner(s) | **\+Confidence** |
| Active players who lost the showdown | **−Confidence** |
| Folded players | 0 |

Scores can go negative — there is no floor.

### Ties

If multiple hands tie for best, **every tied player gains the full Confidence value** — the reward is not split. All tied winners are listed in the winner banner along with the winning hand description (e.g. "Straight, T High").

## Hand rankings

Showdown uses standard poker hand rankings via `pokersolver`:

Royal Flush \> Straight Flush \> Four of a Kind \> Full House \> Flush \> Straight \> Three of a Kind \> Two Pair \> Pair \> High Card.

<Note>
  Because all five community cards are shared and face up, hand strength differences come entirely from two hole cards. Ties are noticeably more common than in Texas Hold'em — the board itself often plays.
</Note>

## Winning the game

The game ends the moment any player's total reaches **50 or more points**, checked after every round-ending score update (both fold-out and showdown paths). That player becomes the game winner.

When the game ends:

1. The winner banner offers a **See Results** button.
2. The Game Over screen shows the final leaderboard, sorted by total score.
3. **Back to Lobby** reloads the app so you can start fresh.

The host can also force the game to end at any time with **End Game**.

## Worked example

Four players; Confidence gets raised to 6; one player folds along the way. At showdown, one hand wins outright:

| Player | Round action | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ava | Raised to 6, best hand | \+6 |
| Ben | Called 6, lost | −6 |
| Cam | Called 6, lost | −6 |
| Dia | Folded at 3 | 0 |

Net points are not zero-sum — with two losers, 12 points were destroyed while 6 were created. High-confidence multiway showdowns drain the table; fold-out wins inflate it.
