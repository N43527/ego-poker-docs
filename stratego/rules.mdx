## The board

A 10×10 grid (100 cells) with two impassable 2×2 **lakes** in the middle of the board, occupying rows 5–6 at columns C–D and G–H. No piece may enter or pass through a lake.

Each player sets up in their own four back rows: Red on rows 7–10, Blue on rows 1–4. The two middle rows (5–6, outside the lakes) start empty.

## The army

Each player fields exactly **40 pieces**:

| Rank | Piece | Count | Movable |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F | Flag | 1 | No |
| B | Bomb | 6 | No |
| 1 | Spy | 1 | Yes |
| 2 | Scout | 8 | Yes |
| 3 | Miner | 5 | Yes |
| 4 | Sergeant | 4 | Yes |
| 5 | Lieutenant | 4 | Yes |
| 6 | Captain | 4 | Yes |
| 7 | Major | 3 | Yes |
| 8 | Colonel | 2 | Yes |
| 9 | General | 1 | Yes |
| 10 | Marshal | 1 | Yes |

<Note>
This variant uses **ascending** rank numbering: 10 (Marshal) is the strongest, 1 (Spy) the weakest. On the board, the Flag renders as 🏴, the Bomb as 💣, and the Spy as `S`.
</Note>

## Movement

- **Standard pieces** move exactly **one square orthogonally** (up, down, left, right). Diagonal movement does not exist.
- **Scouts (2)** slide **any number of squares in a straight line**, like a rook. They are blocked by lakes and by any piece; they may attack the first enemy piece in their path.
- **Flags and Bombs never move.**
- You may move onto an empty square or onto an enemy piece (an attack). You can never move onto your own piece or into a lake.

Players alternate turns, one move per turn, starting with Red.

## Battle resolution

Attacking an enemy piece resolves immediately, in this order:

<Accordion title="1. Defender is the Flag">
The attacker captures the Flag and **wins the game on the spot**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="2. Defender is a Bomb">
The Bomb destroys the attacker — unless the attacker is a **Miner (3)**, who defuses the Bomb and takes its square. Bombs are only ever removed by Miners; they otherwise sit and kill indefinitely.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="3. Spy attacks the Marshal">
If a **Spy (1)** attacks the **Marshal (10)**, the Spy wins. This only works on the attack — if the Marshal attacks the Spy, the Marshal wins by rank as usual.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="4. Rank comparison">
Otherwise the higher rank wins and takes the defender's square. **Equal ranks destroy each other** — both pieces are removed.
</Accordion>

The winner of a battle (if any) occupies the defender's square and is permanently **revealed** to both players. Losing pieces move to the captured panel.

## Winning

The game ends when a player **captures the enemy Flag**. That is the only end condition enforced in live play.

<Note>
The rules module (`strategoRules.js`) also implements a secondary win condition — a player with no legal moves remaining loses — via `checkWinCondition`. The multiplayer service never calls it, so in the deployed game a fully immobilized player is simply stuck; only a Flag capture ends the game.
</Note>

## Quick reference

```text
Move:    1 square orthogonal (Scout: any distance in a line)
Attack:  move onto an enemy piece
Flag     -> attacker wins the game
Bomb     -> kills attacker, unless attacker is Miner (3)
Spy (1)  -> beats Marshal (10) only when attacking
Ties     -> both pieces removed
```
