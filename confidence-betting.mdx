---
title: "Confidence Betting"
description: "The 1–10 confidence system that replaces chips and pots"
---

Ego Poker has no chips, no pot, and no blinds. Every round has a single shared number: **Confidence**, an integer from 1 to 10. It starts each round at **1** and can only go up.

Confidence is simultaneously the bet and the stake — whatever it is when the round ends is exactly how many points change hands.

## The three actions

### Raise

Raising declares a new, higher Confidence. In the UI you pick a value from a 1–10 grid; values at or below the current Confidence are disabled.

Server-side rules enforced by `performAction`:

- The new value must be **strictly greater** than the current Confidence.
- The new value cannot exceed **10**.

A raise does three things:

1. Sets the shared Confidence to your value.
2. Sets your personal round confidence to the same value.
3. **Resets the acted list** to just you — everyone else must respond to the new level before a showdown can happen.

### Call

Calling matches your personal round confidence to the shared Confidence and marks you as having acted at the current level.

<Note>
There is no separate "check" action. Calling at the current level is how you pass the action along without raising — it costs nothing extra, since Confidence is a declaration rather than a chip commitment.
</Note>

### Fold

Folding marks you out of the round and zeroes your round confidence. Folded players:

- Are skipped in the turn rotation.
- Cannot win the round.
- **Lose no points** at showdown, no matter how high Confidence climbed.

## Showdown trigger

After every action, the game checks whether the round should end. A showdown fires when **both** conditions hold for all non-folded players:

1. **All matched** — every active player's round confidence equals the shared Confidence.
2. **All acted** — every active player is in the `currentActors` list, i.e. has called or raised since the most recent raise.

Because a raise wipes `currentActors` down to just the raiser, a raise always reopens the action: play continues around the table until everyone remaining has answered the new level.

If instead all but one player folds, the round ends immediately without a showdown — the survivor collects the Confidence value uncontested.

## Strategy implications

- **Confidence is a ratchet.** It never decreases within a round, so an early raise to 10 forces everyone to either match maximal stakes or fold.
- **Perfect information on the board.** All five community cards are face up from the start, so the only unknowns are hole cards. Confidence is a pure statement about your two cards.
- **Folding is free.** Since folders never lose points, bluff-heavy raising is punished only when someone calls you down.
- **Symmetric risk at showdown.** Callers stand to lose exactly what the winner gains, so calling a high Confidence is as bold as raising it.
